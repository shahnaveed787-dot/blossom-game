Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we handle and how we keep Blossom Game safe for everyone — especially children.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Blossom Game ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit our website and play the Blossom Word Game (the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information we collect

Blossom Game is designed to be played without creating an account, and we aim to collect as little information as possible. Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

  • Information you provide: If you contact us through our contact form or by email, we receive the details you send us, such as your name, email address and message.
  • Automatically collected data: Like most websites, our servers and analytics tools may record limited technical information such as browser type, device type, general location (country/region) and pages visited.
  • Local storage: The game may store simple preferences or progress in your browser's local storage on your own device. This information stays on your device.

3. How we use information

We use the limited information we handle to:

  • Operate, maintain and improve the Service;
  • Respond to your questions, feedback and support requests;
  • Understand general usage trends so we can make the game better;
  • Keep the Service safe, secure and functioning properly.

4. Cookies & analytics

We may use cookies or similar technologies, and privacy-friendly analytics, to understand how the Service is used and to improve performance. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features but you can still play the game.

5. Children's privacy

Blossom Game is family-friendly and intended to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not require children to register or provide personal details to play. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the Service (for example, hosting or analytics), or where required by law. These providers are expected to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us and we will respond in line with applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us or email support@blossomgamez.com.
