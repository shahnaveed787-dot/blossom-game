Solver guide

How Blossom Game Solver Works?

A Blossom Game solver works by analyzing the letters in the puzzle and finding possible words that follow the game's rules. It takes the seven available letters, identifies the required center letter, and searches through a dictionary database to find valid combinations.

The solver checks each possible word to make sure it contains the center letter, uses only the available letters, and meets the minimum word length requirement. It then filters out invalid words, duplicates, and words that are not accepted by the game dictionary.

Advanced Blossom solvers also calculate scores by ranking words based on length, bonus words, and pangrams (words that use all seven letters). The final result is usually a list of possible answers organized from shortest to longest, helping players discover hidden words and improve their daily puzzle score.
