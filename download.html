Download Blossom Game

Save the free word puzzle on your phone. It opens like an app, with no store account and no extra fees.

On iPhone or iPad, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen.

Android

Open this page in Chrome, tap Download game on your phone, then confirm Install. Blossom appears on your home screen and launches the daily puzzle.

iPhone and iPad

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Blossom icon is added next to your other apps.

Already installed?

Tap the Blossom icon on your phone, or play in the browser.
